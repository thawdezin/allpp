Privacy Policy for DD Baydin

Last updated: June 12, 2026

Thank you for using DD Baydin — your premium guide to Myanmar Traditional Astrology. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your data while you use our app.

1. Data We Collect

1.1 Birth Date (Local Only)

DD Baydin allows you to enter your birth date for astrology calculations (Mahabote, ThetYauk/Age Destiny, and Zodiac readings). Your birth date is stored exclusively on your device using local app storage (SharedPreferences). It is never transmitted to any server.

1.2 App Preferences (Local Only)

Your preferences, including dark mode setting and language choice (English / Myanmar), are stored locally on your device using SharedPreferences.

1.3 Advertising Identifier (iOS Only)

DD Baydin uses Apple's AppTrackingTransparency (ATT) framework to request permission to access your device's advertising identifier (IDFA). This identifier is used solely for delivering personalized advertisements relevant to your interests in astrology, tarot, and fortune-telling, and to help sustain our free daily horoscopes and features.

  • You will be prompted to grant or deny this permission when you first launch the app.
  • If you deny permission, the IDFA will not be accessed, and you will see non-personalized ads.
  • You can change this permission at any time in your device's Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

1.4 Analytics Data

We use Firebase Analytics (Google) to collect anonymous, aggregated usage data to understand how the app is used and to improve its features. This data does not include any personally identifiable information.

  • Screen views and feature usage patterns
  • Crash and error reports (via Firebase Crashlytics, if enabled)
  • Device model, OS version, and app version

2. How We Use Your Data

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • Core Functionality: To perform astrology calculations (Mahabote, ThetYauk, Tarot, Zodiac, Min Thein Kha) based on your birth date and selections.
  • Personalization: To remember your language and theme preferences across sessions.
  • Advertising: To serve relevant advertisements that help keep the app free. With your consent, ads may be personalized based on your advertising identifier.
  • Analytics: To analyze anonymous usage patterns and improve app performance and features.

3. Data Sharing

We may share non-personal, anonymous data with the following third-party services:

  • Google Firebase Analytics: For anonymous usage analysis. Please refer to Google's Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Ad Networks: If you have granted ATT permission, your advertising identifier may be shared with ad networks for personalized advertising.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to any third party.

4. Data Storage & Security

  • All astrology data (birth dates, preferences) are stored locally on your device only.
  • No account registration or sign-in is required to use DD Baydin.
  • No personal data is stored on any remote server.

5. Data Retention

Your local data (birth date, preferences) is retained on your device until you either:

  • Clear the app's data through your device settings, or
  • Uninstall the app from your device.

Analytics data retained by Google Firebase is subject to Google's data retention policies.

6. Children's Privacy

DD Baydin is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately.

7. Your Rights

You have the following rights regarding your data:

  • Access and Control: All your personal data stored by the app is accessible to you directly within the app or through your device settings.
  • Deletion: You can delete all locally stored data by uninstalling the app. Analytics data deletion requests can be sent to our contact email.
  • Tracking Consent: You can grant or withdraw consent for tracking at any time through your device's Privacy settings.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
