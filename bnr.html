Privacy Policy for DD BitePu

Last updated: April 10, 2026

Thank you for choosing DD BitePu. We are committed to protecting your privacy while providing you with the best transit experience in Yangon. This policy outlines how we handle data to improve our services and ensure application security.

1. Information Collection and Use

While using our Application, we may collect certain information to provide and improve the service:

  • Device Identification: We generate a non-personally identifiable, persistent unique signature based on hashed hardware metadata and network context. This allows us to maintain data consistency and provide a seamless experience across app reinstalls without requiring a user login.
  • Location Data: With your explicit permission, the Application collects precise location data to provide bus navigation, real-time stop proximity, and route optimization. This includes background location collection to ensure transit alerts and route tracking function correctly even when the app is not actively in use.
  • Usage & Telemetry: We collect anonymized telemetry data, including movement speed, displacement, and interaction patterns, to analyze urban mobility trends and improve the accuracy of our bus transit database.

2. Data Analytics & Security Research

The information collected is utilized for the following core purposes:

  • Urban Mobility Analysis: Aggregated and anonymized data helps us identify traffic patterns and optimize bus route recommendations for the public interest.
  • Security and Anti-Scraping: We monitor for stationary anomalies and non-standard usage patterns to protect our proprietary transit data from unauthorized automated scraping and to ensure the integrity of our service.
  • Service Optimization: Usage metrics help our technical team improve app stability and performance.

3. Third-Party Services

We utilize trusted third-party providers to enhance our functionality. These providers may process data according to their own privacy policies:

  • Google Maps API: For map rendering and geocoding.
  • Cloud Firestore: For secure, encrypted storage of synchronized transit telemetry.
  • Firebase Analytics: To understand general user engagement trends.

4. Data Retention and Disclosure

We retain anonymized data for as long as necessary to fulfill the research and analytical purposes described in this policy. We do not sell, trade, or rent personal identification information to third parties. We may disclose information only when required by law or to protect the safety and rights of our community.

5. Your Rights and Choices

You have full control over your data. You may:

  • Disable location tracking at any time via your device settings.
  • Request information regarding the data we hold or request its deletion.
  • Opt-out of optional telemetry collection by adjusting app preferences where available.

6. Security Measures

We employ industry-standard encryption and procedural safeguards to protect your data from unauthorized access. However, please be aware that no transmission over the internet or electronic storage is completely infallible.
