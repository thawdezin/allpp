Privacy Policy for Bus Nexus Rader

Last updated: August 28, 2024

Thank you for using Bus Nexus Rader! Your privacy is important to us, and this Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information when you use the Bus Nexus Rader app:

  • Personal Information: Such as your name, email address, or phone number, if you voluntarily provide it (e.g., during feedback or support requests).
  • Location Data: To show bus lines, routes, and nearby bus stops, we may collect your location with your consent.
  • Usage Data: Information on how you interact with the app, such as pages viewed, search queries, and features used.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information collected for the following purposes:

  • To provide accurate bus routes, schedules, and navigation services.
  • To improve app performance, user experience, and new features.
  • To provide customer support and respond to your feedback.
  • To ensure the safety and security of the app.

3. Third-Party Services

We may use third-party services to enhance our app. These services may collect data to provide their features:

  • Google Maps API for displaying maps and bus routes.
  • Firebase Analytics to understand app usage and improve performance.

These third-party services operate under their own privacy policies.

4. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to others. Your data may only be shared:

  • With your consent.
  • To comply with legal obligations.
  • To protect the safety and rights of our users.

5. Security

We prioritize the security of your data. We use industry-standard measures to protect your personal information; however, no method of electronic storage or transmission is 100% secure.

6. Your Privacy Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access or update your personal information.
  • Request deletion of your personal data.
  • Withdraw consent for location access at any time.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with the updated date. Please check periodically for updates.
